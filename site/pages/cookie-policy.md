This website uses as few cookies as it can. One remembers the choice you made
in the cookie banner. Google Analytics, which tells us which pages are read,
sets cookies **only if you allow the analytics category**. There is no
advertising and no marketing tracking.

You can change or withdraw your choice at any time:

<p><button type="button" data-cookie-settings class="button">Manage cookies</button></p>

The banner opens by itself for visitors in the European Economic Area and the
United Kingdom, where the law requires consent first. Visitors elsewhere are
not asked, and Google Analytics stays without cookies for them too unless they
allow it with **Manage cookies**.

## Strictly necessary

Always on; they do not need consent.

| Cookie | Purpose | Retention |
|---|---|---|
| `ssg_consent` | Remembers your choice in the cookie banner | 180 days |

## Analytics: off until you allow it

| Service | Cookies | Provider | Retention |
|---|---|---|---|
| Google Analytics 4 | `_ga`, `_ga_QX8Y8L1B20` | Google Ireland Ltd ([privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)) | 2 years |

Until you allow analytics, Google Analytics runs in consent mode with storage
denied: it sets no cookies and sends only anonymous, cookieless signals that
Google uses to estimate visits.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR and the UK GDPR you can withdraw consent as easily as you gave
it, with **Manage cookies** above. Withdrawing stops the cookies from being set
from then on; to remove ones already stored, clear this site's cookies in your
browser.

Questions: open an issue on
